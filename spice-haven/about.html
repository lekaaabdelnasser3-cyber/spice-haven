ABOUT

Spice Haven was born in 2010 from a simple belief: great Indian food should be accessible, authentic, and deeply nourishing. Chef Arjun Mehta and his family started the kitchen, drawing on recipes passed down through three generations and refining them with the best local produce and spices sourced directly from India.

Every dish on our menu tells a story of regions, rituals, and the remarkable variety of Indian cuisine. From the slow-braised kormas of the north to the coastal seafood curries of Kerala, we bring the full range of flavour to your table.

We're proud to have served over 50,000 guests in the heart of San Francisco, with a 4.9-star rating and three years running as one of the Bay Area's top Indian restaurants. Our doors are always open, so come and find your new favourite dish.
